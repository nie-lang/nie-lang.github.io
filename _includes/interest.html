Research Interests

My research focuses on learning-based image warping, multi-view geometry, and panoramic scene understanding. The specific intersts are listed below:

1) Single-view warping:

Wide-angle rectification; image rectangling; rolling shutter correction; rotation correction

1) Multi-view geometry:

Image/Video stitching; homography estimation; video stabilization; visual localization; 3D reconstruction

3) Panoramic scene understanding:

Panoramic object detection; semantic segmentation; depth estimation; layout estimation; keypoint detection
